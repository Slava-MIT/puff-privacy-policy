Privacy Policy for PUFF

**Last updated: November 17, 2026**

PUFF is an Android game developed by Slava-MIT.

1. Personal Information

PUFF does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The application does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal identifying information.

2. AI Learning and Local Storage

PUFF includes an AI learning system that adapts aspects of gameplay.

The application stores learning information locally on the user's device to support this functionality.

This information is stored in the application's private storage and is not intentionally transmitted to the developer.

3. Internet Access and Radio Streaming

PUFF uses an internet connection to provide access to Bloomberg radio streaming.

When users access the radio stream, the external streaming provider may receive technical connection information, such as an IP address.

The handling of this information is governed by the streaming provider's privacy practices.

4. Advertising and Analytics

PUFF does not intentionally integrate advertising networks or third-party analytics services.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not intentionally sell or share locally stored AI learning information with third parties.

6. Data Deletion

Users can remove locally stored application information by clearing PUFF's application data or uninstalling the application, subject to Android's data-management behavior.

### 7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's functionality or data practices change.
